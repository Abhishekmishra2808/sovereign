How the comparison was made

Same conditions.
One thread per solver, pinned CPU cores, same 300-second wall-clock limit and MILP gap target of 1e-6.
Independent checker.
Bounds, scaled row violations, integrality and objective values are recomputed outside both solvers.
Published references.
Netlib readme, MIPLIB solution values and Maros–Mészáros 00README.QP are used with documented tolerances.
No hidden cleanup.
TIMEOUT, FAILED and numerical-error results stay in the report; they are not counted as solved.

What the evidence says

Strength Sovereign is particularly competitive on convex QP and large Kennington LPs, while maintaining zero independently detected wrong answers in the frozen 319-instance run.

Limitation HiGHS remains stronger on MILP coverage and faster on the full Netlib set.

Open cases pilot87, LISWET1 and several weak-relaxation MILPs remain difficult. These are reported as engineering targets, not hidden.

Reproduce The detailed source tables are in benchmarks/reports/COVERAGE.md and the harnesses under benchmarks/runners/.
